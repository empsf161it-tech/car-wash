About Us

Our story, mission, and the team behind premium car care

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Car Wash began with a simple vision: to provide premium car care services that exceed customer expectations. What started as a small neighborhood car wash has grown into a trusted name in auto detailing.

Our founder, passionate about cars and committed to excellence, recognized a gap in the market for truly professional car care services. We built our business on three core principles: quality, convenience, and environmental responsibility.

Today, we serve thousands of customers who trust us with their vehicles. From daily drivers to luxury cars, we treat every vehicle with the same level of care and attention to detail.
