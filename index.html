Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What is included in a Full Detail service?

Our Full Detail service includes a complete exterior wash, paint correction, interior deep cleaning, vacuuming, window cleaning, and leather conditioning. It provides a showroom finish for your vehicle.

How long does a Ceramic Coating last?

Depending on the package you choose and how well you maintain your vehicle, our ceramic coatings can last anywhere from 1 to 5 years. We provide care instructions to help maximize its longevity.

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

While we accept walk-ins for our Express Wash, we highly recommend booking an appointment for detailing services and ceramic coatings to ensure we have dedicated time for your vehicle.

What products do you use?

We use only premium, eco-friendly, and industry-leading products. This ensures that your car gets the best care without any harsh chemicals that might damage the paint or the environment.
